I'm a Frontend Developer who cares about the details users feel but rarely notice: pages that load faster, data that updates by itself, and interfaces that stay clean no matter how much is happening underneath.

Working side by side with backend developers on REST, GraphQL and WebSocket contracts, I shipped 15+ production features there.
