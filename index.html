Hi, I'm Bohdan. I'm a Frontend Developer from Ukraine, now living in Wrocław, and I like building things people actually enjoy using.


What pulls me into frontend is that it's the part of the product people touch. A page that loads a second faster, a table that updates on its own, a button that's exactly where you expect it to be: nobody writes a thank-you note for those, but everyone feels the difference. That's the kind of work I enjoy most. Day to day I work with React · TypeScript and Redux Toolkit.


So far I've worked in two very different teams. At TenThousand I helped build a CRM for shipments and orders, where I learned how much a good conversation with a backend developer can save you later. At GlobalLogic I worked on an internal platform used by several teams to plan and track their work, and made components other teams could reuse instead of rewriting.


Outside of work I keep building side projects, from a Spotify-style music app to a React Native app for finding places nearby, and yes, this Persona-themed portfolio too. I speak Ukrainian, Russian, Polish and English, so I'm comfortable in pretty much any team here. If you'd like to see how I write code, it's all on github.com/StepBohdan.
